## 🧩 About

**Monster Hunter Rise Companion** is a curated list of Monster Hunter Rise open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, monster tracker, HUD notes
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — item catalog, loadout manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — hunt layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Hunt Helpers
- Monster Tracker — follow hunt targets on the map overlay
- Show Weak Points — visualize hitzone notes for study
- Replay Notes — record and review hunt runs
- Playback Speed — adjust practice playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎒 Catalog & Loadouts
- Item Catalog — browse items, materials, and decorations
- Custom Loadout Notes — organize armor and weapon sets
- Inventory Overlay — improved visibility of pouch contents

### 🏋️ Practice Layout
- Practice Mode Notes — segment study on any hunt layout
- Wirebug Control Notes — manually review traversal routes
- Combo Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the training area
